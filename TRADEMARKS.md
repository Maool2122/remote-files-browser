# Names and pictures

The **code and the documents** of this repository are under the [MIT licence](LICENSE): use them, change them, and share them, also in your own products, as the licence says.

The licence does **not** give you the right to use the **names and the look** that identify this project and its publisher:

- the names **Rocuo**, **Remote Files Browser** and **Remote Files Browser FTP by Rocuo**;
- the Rocuo **logo**, the **icon** of the extension, and the **pictures** in `docs/images/`, which show the extension and carry its look.

This is so that a person who installs "Remote Files Browser FTP by Rocuo" gets the extension and the helper that Rocuo publishes, and not a copy that only looks like it. The same rule is why most open-source projects keep their names for themselves. The extension itself is not open source and is not covered by the MIT licence of this repository.

## What you may do

- **Fork it, change it, build on it, and publish your result.** That is the point of an open repository.
- **Say what your work is based on.** "Based on the helper of Remote Files Browser FTP by Rocuo" is welcome, and so is a link to this repository.
- **Write about it, review it, teach it.** Use the name and the pictures in articles, videos, posts and lists to say which project you mean, as long as it is clear that you are not the publisher.
- **Build it for yourself** and run it on your own computer ([helper/docs/BUILDING.md](helper/docs/BUILDING.md)). A copy you built for your own use keeps its name for your own use.

## What you may not do

- **Publish a changed or rebuilt copy under the same name or with the same icon**, anywhere, so that people can take it for the one Rocuo publishes. Give your version a **name of its own** and, if you ship an extension, an **icon of its own**. The names that a fork changes are listed in [docs/WHY-OPEN-SOURCE.md](docs/WHY-OPEN-SOURCE.md#10-if-you-want-to-make-your-own-thing-from-it).
- **Say or suggest that Rocuo made, checked or endorses your version.**
- **Use the names Rocuo or Remote Files Browser as the name of a company, a product or a domain.**

If your version ships its own extension, it should also have **its own privacy policy** and its own contact, and the helper program (`rocuo-files-helper`) of a fork should be built from your source, with your own extension address on its list (`install --extension-id`, [helper/docs/INSTALLING.md](helper/docs/INSTALLING.md#installing-for-a-team)). The helper that Rocuo publishes starts only for the extension that Rocuo publishes.

## Questions

If you are not sure whether something is fine, write to **info@rocuo.com** before you publish. We would much rather say yes than find out later.
